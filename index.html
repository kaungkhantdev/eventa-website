Why Eventa

Everything Your Team Needs To Run Events Faster

From the first draft to the last refund, every part of an event lives in one place. No spreadsheets passed around and no tools stitched together, just one workspace your whole team can trust.

See it in action

Ticket Sales

Every ticket type shows its price, its status and how many have sold, so you can see what's on sale, scheduled or about to sell out.

Attendee Directory

Search everyone who has registered for your events by name or email, filter to new, checked-in or VIP guests, and see how many events each person has joined.

Automated Messages

Confirmations, receipts, 24-hour reminders and waitlist offers go out on their own by email or SMS. Switch each one on or off, edit its wording, and check the delivery log.

Use cases

Built For Every Kind Of Event, Tailored To How You Work

From a 40-seat workshop to a 4,000-seat concert hall, Eventa fits the way your team sells tickets and runs the day.

Conferences & Summits

Plan the agenda day by day, with keynotes, talks, workshops and panels, each with its time, room and speaker. Then publish a polished event page from one of four templates.

  • Agenda, sessions and speaker line-up
  • Share links, social buttons and a printable QR flyer

Concerts & Venues

Set out your rows and seats once. Buyers choose a ticket type, pick their own seats on the seat map, where booked seats are greyed out, and pay by card or PromptPay.

  • Reserved seating, up to 8 seats per booking
  • QR ticket scanning at the door

Workshops & Meetups

Approve or decline registrations by hand when places are limited, keep a waitlist for when someone cancels, and send a feedback survey after the event.

  • Approval queue and waitlists
  • Post-event surveys and ratings
