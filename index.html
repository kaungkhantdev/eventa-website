Why Eventa

Everything Your Team Needs To Run Events Faster

From the first draft to the last refund, every part of an event lives in one place. No spreadsheets passed around and no tools stitched together, just one workspace your whole team can trust.

See it in action

Ticket Sales

Every ticket type shows its price, its status and how many have sold, so you can see what's on sale, scheduled or about to sell out.

Attendee Directory

Search everyone who has registered for your events by name or email, filter to new, checked-in or VIP guests, and see how many events each person has joined.

Automated Messages

Confirmations, receipts, 24-hour reminders and waitlist offers go out on their own by email or SMS. Switch each one on or off, edit its wording, and check the delivery log.
